03 / TRY IT

Meet Valkyrie.

Valkyrie is our IGCSE question-answering assistant. Ask it by paper reference like 0625/42/M/J/23 Q4, or just describe a question in plain words — it searches a library of real past papers, finds the matching official mark scheme, and walks you through the full answer, mark by mark.

Retrieval, not guessing

Every answer is grounded in an actual mark scheme — Valkyrie shows its source, so it explains how marks are really awarded rather than inventing them.

Two ways to ask

Look up an exact paper and question by its code, or ask conceptually (“how do I find resultant force?”) and let Valkyrie find the closest match.

Built by us

We assembled the question library, the search pipeline and the tutor that turns barebones mark schemes into clear, full explanations.

Try asking by paper reference: 0625/42/M/J/23 Q4

Powered by our retrieval pipeline + Gemini, hosted on Hugging Face.
